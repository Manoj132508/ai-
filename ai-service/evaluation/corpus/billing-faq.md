# Ledgerly — Billing FAQ

## What plans are available?

Ledgerly offers three plans: **Starter, Growth, and Scale**. You can change plan
at any time, and a change takes effect on your next billing date.

## Can I get a refund?

Refunds are available **within 30 days** of a purchase. After 30 days, charges
are non-refundable but you can cancel to stop future billing.

## When are invoices issued?

Invoices are issued on the **first business day of each month** and emailed to
the account's billing contact.

## Which currencies do you support?

Billing is available in **USD, GBP, and EUR**. The currency is fixed when the
account is created and cannot be changed afterwards.
