# Acme Corp — Employee Handbook (excerpt)

## Leaving the company

An employee who resigns must give at least **30 days' written notice** to their
line manager. Notice given verbally does not start the notice period.

## Annual leave

Full-time employees accrue **20 days of paid annual leave** per calendar year.
Unused leave may be carried over, up to a maximum of 5 days, into the following
year.

## Expenses

Expense claims must be submitted within **60 days** of the expense being
incurred. Claims submitted after 60 days will not be reimbursed except with
director approval.

## Probation

New employees serve a probation period of **three months**, during which either
party may end the employment with one week's notice.
