# Acme Corp — Incident Response Policy (excerpt)

## Reporting a breach

A suspected data breach must be reported to the security team **within 1 hour**
of discovery, by phone or the incident hotline — not by email.

## Encryption

All customer data is encrypted at rest using **AES-256**. Data in transit is
protected with TLS 1.2 or higher.

## Backups

Backups are taken **every 6 hours** and retained for 30 days. A restore is
tested at least once per quarter.

## Penetration testing

An external penetration test is conducted **annually** by an independent firm,
and any critical findings are remediated within 30 days.
