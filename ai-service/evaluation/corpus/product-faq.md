# Ledgerly — Frequently Asked Questions

## Is there a free trial?

Yes. Ledgerly offers a **14-day free trial** with no credit card required. You
can cancel at any time during the trial and will not be charged.

## Can I export my data?

You can export all of your data as **CSV or JSON** at any time from the account
settings page. Exports are not rate-limited.

## What are your support hours?

Support is available **Monday to Friday, 9am to 6pm** (UK time). Priority-plan
customers also have access to weekend email support.

## What does Ledgerly integrate with?

Ledgerly integrates with **Stripe, QuickBooks, and Xero**. Additional
integrations are added based on customer demand.
