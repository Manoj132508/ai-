# Acme Corp — Information Security Policy (excerpt)

## Passwords

Account passwords must be **at least 12 characters** long and must not reuse any
of the previous five passwords. Passwords do not expire on a fixed schedule.

## Multi-factor authentication

**Multi-factor authentication is required for all administrator accounts.**
Standard user accounts are strongly encouraged to enable it but are not
required to.

## Data retention

Customer data is retained for **24 months** after account closure, after which
it is permanently deleted. Backups are purged on the same schedule.

## Access reviews

Access rights to production systems are reviewed **quarterly**. Any access no
longer required is revoked within five working days of the review.
