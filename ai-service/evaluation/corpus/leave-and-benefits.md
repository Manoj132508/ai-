# Acme Corp — Leave and Benefits (excerpt)

## Sick leave

Employees are entitled to **10 days of paid sick leave** per calendar year. A
doctor's note is required for any absence longer than five consecutive days.

## Pension

The company matches employee pension contributions **up to 5% of salary**.
Matching begins after the three-month probation period.

## Working hours

Standard working hours are **37.5 hours per week**, typically worked between
9am and 5:30pm with an unpaid lunch break.

## Remote work

Employees may work remotely **up to 3 days per week**, subject to their
manager's agreement. Fully remote arrangements are handled case by case.
